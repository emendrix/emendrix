Privacy notice for email alerts

This notice covers the optional account service of this site: the email alerts for watched acts and provisions, and the personal feed. The rest of the site sets no cookie and stores nothing about its readers.

What is held

  • Your email address, and when you signed up, last signed in and last used the service.
  • Your watchlists: their names, the acts and provisions they watch, and how often you want email. What you watch can reveal what you or your organisation works on.
  • The record that you confirmed your address, and which version of this notice you accepted.

Nothing else: no name, no payment details, no tracking, and no analytics.

  • To keep the service secure and its email deliverable, including stopping mail to an address that bounced or reported it as unwanted: the controller's legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
